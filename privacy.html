Privacy Policy for Free Games Online & Game Forum

Last revised: October 2026. How games.utility.forum safeguards player privacy across all browser games and community channels.

1. Privacy-First Online Gaming Principles

At games.utility.forum, we believe that playing free games online should not come at the expense of your personal data. We do not sell player information, we do not partner with invasive third-party ad networks, and we strictly minimize data collection to what is essential to run our browser games, audio synthesizers, and game forum features.

2. What Information We Process

A. Browser Local Storage (Scores & Audio Settings)

To maintain your gameplay experience without forcing you to sign up for an account, our games utilize your browser's standard localStorage API to remember:

  • Audio HUD Preferences: Mute toggle state (guf_audio_muted) and volume level.
  • Visual Theme Choice: Whether you prefer Day mode or Night mode (guf_theme).
  • Anonymous Guest Identifier: A randomly generated string (e.g. player_7b2f9) so your scores are uniquely counted without requiring an email address.
  • Local Offline Saves: Cached personal high scores so games remain playable even without an internet connection.

B. Cloud Leaderboard Data (Public Handles & Scores)

When you play games connected to our cloud database or participate in the game forum, the following public information is stored:

  • Leaderboard Entries: Your chosen player handle, your achieved numerical score, the game ID, and the timestamp of completion.
  • Game Progression States: Non-sensitive progression states (such as unlocked color schemes or difficulty stages).

C. Server Access Logs (Security & Traffic Optimization)

Standard HTTP requests generate technical log entries containing IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps. These logs are stored transiently by our hosting and edge delivery infrastructure strictly for traffic routing, DDoS prevention, and service stability.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Unlike mainstream commercial game portals, we do not embed:

  • Third-party behavioral tracking pixels or conversion cookies.
  • Cross-site advertising networks (such as programmatic ad exchanges).
  • Data broker analytics libraries.
No Ads Guarantee: Our games are built for enjoyment and community discussion on our game forum, not for serving intrusive advertisements.

4. Player Data Control & Deletion Rights

You remain in full control of your gaming data:

  • Clearing Local Data: You can wipe all local high scores and preferences instantly at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies for games.utility.forum.
  • Requesting Leaderboard Score Removal: If you wish to delete a specific submitted score from the cloud database, contact our moderators through the game forum at utility.forum with your player handle.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Our free games online are suitable for general audiences. We do not intentionally collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Policy Updates & Game Forum Inquiries

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be published directly to this page with an updated timestamp. For questions or suggestions, connect with our team on the game forum at utility.forum.
