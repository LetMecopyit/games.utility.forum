DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How games.utility.forum respects and protects your gameplay data.

1. Overview & Core Commitment

At games.utility.forum ("we", "our", or "the platform"), we believe in privacy-first web design. We do not sell user data, we do not utilize invasive advertising networks, and we collect only the minimal data necessary to deliver interactive games, synthesized audio, and persistent scoreboards.

2. Information We Process

A. Local Browser Storage (LocalStorage)

To maintain your gameplay state without mandatory registration, we utilize your web browser's localStorage API to store:

  • Audio Preferences: Whether background synth music is muted or active (guf_audio_muted) and volume level.
  • Anonymous Guest Identifier: A randomly generated string (e.g. player_x9a2b) enabling progress saving without personal identity.
  • Local High Scores & Saves: Offline copies of high scores and level progression so games work seamlessly without network connectivity.

B. Supabase Cloud Database Storage

When connected to our backend infrastructure hosted on Supabase, the following game-related items are synchronized:

  • Leaderboard Entries: Your chosen public handle or guest pseudonym, the game title (e.g. neon-snake), your earned score, and a submission timestamp.
  • Game Progression Saves: Non-sensitive game configuration states, such as unlocked game levels or selected themes.

C. Hosting & Edge Network Logs (Vercel)

Our application is hosted on Vercel. Standard HTTP server access logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps) are processed transiently by Vercel for security, rate-limiting, and network optimization.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Our platform relies on the following infrastructure providers:

  • Vercel Inc.: Static file hosting, DNS routing, and edge caching.
  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database for leaderboard data and save records.
  • Google Fonts: Web font typography delivery.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because we do not mandate personal accounts or capture email addresses for basic gameplay:

  • Clearing Local Data: You may at any time delete all locally stored game scores and preferences by clearing your browser's site data/cookies for games.utility.forum.
  • Leaderboard Removal: If you wish to remove a score submitted under a specific handle from our Supabase tables, contact the site administrator with your submission details for immediate removal.
No Commercial Tracking: We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking pixels, cross-site analytics cookies, or behavioral advertising brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are designed for general public entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this page with an updated timestamp. For questions or privacy inquiries, contact the team via our community portal at utility.forum.
